\needspace{8\baselineskip}\cvsection{Research Interests}

\begin{cvparagraph}
I am a Senior Researcher at the Human-Inspired AI Research Institute, Korea University, where I founded and lead \textbf{KUDoc}, a document AI research group focused on multimodal parsing, retrieval, and grounded QA. Co-advised by \textbf{Prof.~Jaehyung Seo} and \textbf{Prof.~Heuiseok Lim}, my research develops structure-grounded multimodal AI systems that transform unstructured documents and videos into structured evidence for retrieval, reasoning, and verification.

\medskip
My work has led to \textbf{15 publications}, including first-author papers at \textbf{NeurIPS, ACL, CVPR, and EMNLP Main}, along with \textbf{5 patents} and \textbf{7 awards}, among them \textbf{3 Outstanding Paper awards}.
\end{cvparagraph}
