Joongmin Shin

Senior Researcher, Human-Inspired AI Research, Korea University · Founder & Lead, KUDoc
Working with Prof. Heuiseok Lim and Prof. Jaehyung Seo
B.S. & M.S., Pusan National University (Advisor: Prof. Hyuk-Chul Kwon)

My research uses structure-based reasoning to address real-world and industrial problems, with a focus on making AI genuinely useful to people. My master’s research in parsing gave me this structural perspective, while my later work in human-inspired AI clarified what that perspective should ultimately serve. I have since developed a coherent research program spanning multimodal and agentic AI.

Looking ahead, I aim to study how AI systems should represent, select, update, and verify information so that they remain useful and reliable as they reason over complex multimodal inputs and engage in extended interactions. Over the longer term, I am interested in extending these principles to world models and embodied agents in robotics.

  • Structure-based Reasoning
  • Multimodality
  • Agentic AI
