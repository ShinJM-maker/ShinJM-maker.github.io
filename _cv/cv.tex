\needspace{8\baselineskip}\cvsection{Research Interests}

\begin{cvparagraph}
{\RaggedRight I am a Senior Researcher at the \cvlink{https://hiai.korea.ac.kr/}{Human-Inspired AI Research Institute}, Korea University, where I work with \textbf{Prof.~\cvlink{https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=HMTkz7oAAAAJ}{Heuiseok~Lim}} and \textbf{Prof.~\cvlink{https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=V8bFAUIAAAAJ}{Jaehyung~Seo}} and lead \cvlink{https://kudocai.github.io/index.html}{\textbf{KUDoc}}. I received my B.S. in Information and Computer Engineering and my M.S. in Artificial Intelligence (\textbf{Advisor: Prof.~\cvlink{https://www.researchgate.net/profile/Hyuk-Chul-Kwon}{Hyuk-Chul~Kwon}}) from Pusan National University.\par}

\smallskip
My research uses \textbf{structure-based reasoning} to address \textbf{real-world and industrial problems}, with a focus on making AI genuinely useful to people. My master's research in parsing gave me this structural perspective, while my later work in human-inspired AI clarified how that perspective could serve practical human needs. I have since developed a coherent research program spanning multimodal and agentic AI.

\smallskip
Looking ahead, I aim to study how AI systems should represent, select, update, and verify information so that they remain reliable as they reason over complex multimodal inputs and engage in extended interactions. Over the longer term, I am interested in extending these principles to world models and embodied agents in robotics.

\smallskip
My research has yielded \textbf{14 publications}, including \textbf{6 first-author papers} at \textbf{NeurIPS}, \textbf{ACL}, \textbf{CVPR}, and \textbf{EMNLP}; these include main-track papers at all four venues, with my ACL 2026 paper selected for \textbf{oral presentation}. I also hold \textbf{5 patents} and have received \textbf{7 awards}, including \textbf{3 paper awards}. KUDoc-affiliated projects have produced \textbf{15 publications}, \textbf{3 technology transfers}, and multiple industry collaborations.
\end{cvparagraph}
