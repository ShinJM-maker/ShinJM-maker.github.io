Joongmin Shin

Senior Researcher, Human-Inspired AI Research Institute, Korea University
Founder & Lead, KUDoc

Co-advised by Prof. Jaehyung Seo and Prof. Heuiseok Lim, I develop structure-grounded multimodal AI systems that transform unstructured documents and videos into structured evidence for retrieval, reasoning, and verification. I founded and lead KUDoc, a document AI research group focused on multimodal parsing, retrieval, and grounded QA.

  • Structure-Grounded Multimodal Reasoning
  • Evidence-Grounded Information Retrieval & Generation
  • Agentic AI
  • Knowledge Graphs and Neural-Symbolic Reasoning

I am seeking Ph.D. opportunities in multimodal reasoning, structured memory, world models, and planning for future agents. Please reach out via email.
